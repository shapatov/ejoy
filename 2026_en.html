About eJOY

The European Junior Olympiad in Ynformatics (eJOY) is a prestigious contest for young talents in ynformatics. The contest is known for its completely "standard" and "ordinary" problems.

Contest rules

  • Solutions are written in the C++ language, unless the task requires otherwise.
  • Using the internet or any external help is strictly prohibited during the contest, unless the task requires it.
  • The contest is held online over 2 days, in which contestants get a total of 6-7 tasks.
  • Solutions are graded automatically by the grading system.

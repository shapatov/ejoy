Analysis of task statement

Of course, this task has many different ideas. One possible solution is the task for the sum of 2 numbers, but this gives
few points, depending on the implementation. One of the provided author solutions is from the task monopoly2 from IATI, 
which gives the maximum points among the solutions tested by the author, and it is not determined exactly how many, because
the points depend on the mood of the checker. A solution for 100 points has not been found so far.

For the curious, the checker works through the API of NVIDIA NIM and uses the free model qwen/qwen3-next-80b-a3b-instruct.
